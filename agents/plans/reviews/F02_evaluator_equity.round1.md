ROUND 1 REVIEW of `agents/plans/F02_evaluator_equity.md`

B1 [major] §3.3 river partial enumeration / §8.2 “slightly biased” — A weighted average over a uniformly sampled subset can have severe bias, contradicting §1's unbiased estimate requirement. With 128 positive-weight pairs, one winning pair of weight 1,000,000 and 127 losing pairs of weight 1, exact equity is 0.999873. After 64 uniformly selected pairs, expected estimated equity is only 0.499969 because the dominant pair is included half the time. — Use a weighted iid sampling estimator with a plain mean for budget-limited river evaluation, or another estimator with a justified unbiasedness guarantee. Retain exact enumeration when it completes. Add a concentrated-range, truncated-budget test across seeds.

B2 [major] §3.3 range preparation and n_eff — Individually finite weights can overflow their cumulative sum and squared-weight sums. Two weights of 1e308 give total=inf, making the bisect target invalid and weighted averages/n_eff potentially NaN. Very small weights can likewise underflow when squared. These are valid inputs under the stated contract. — Rescale positive finite weights by their maximum before computing cumulative sums, weighted averages, or n_eff. Test scale invariance using ordinary, very large, and very small finite weights, and require finite outputs.

B3 [major] §3.3 budget / §4.11 NaN — The stated deadline formula makes deadline NaN for a NaN budget. Deadline comparisons then remain false, so the loop reaches MAX_SAMPLES rather than the promised MIN_SAMPLES. — Normalize NaN and nonpositive budgets before forming the deadline, define infinity handling, and test zero, negative, NaN, and infinite budgets with a fake clock and sample counts.

B4 [major] §8.3 composition for pre-discard equity — deal_samples draws only an opponent pair and permits every other unseen card in the runout. Combining it with equity_vs_samples cannot by itself implement the required opponent-triple discard model. The discarded third card depends on the sampled triple and flop and must remain excluded from that sample's runout. Marginal pair weights cannot generally represent this joint distribution. — Specify that F04 must generate triple-based samples, apply its discard policy using only the triple and flop, and exclude all three opponent cards when generating the runout. It can then pass the kept pair and runout to equity_vs_samples. Correct the claim that the existing deal_samples composition suffices, and add a future integration test for discarded-card exclusion.

B5 [minor] §6B.5 identical results with the same seed — The public equity function stops according to elapsed wall time. Identical seeds and equivalent weight formats can process different sample counts because preparation and execution times vary, producing different Monte Carlo estimates. — Test format equivalence with a fixed sample count, a controlled clock, or a budget guaranteed to reach MAX_SAMPLES. Keep real-time budget measurements separate from exact deterministic equality assertions.

VERDICT: 5 bugs
